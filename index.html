How to play

1. Stand centered and recenter.
2. Step toward the glowing target on the court.
3. Swing through the glowing ball with your virtual racket when the cue turns green. No physical racket needed.

Challenge mode

No target ring, no timing bar, no cues. The ball freezes in the air, then vanishes just before it reaches you. Read its speed and position, step into place, and swing on your own timing. Points come from hits, timing accuracy and streaks. Every 60 points raises the level (up to 10): the ball gets faster, hides sooner, lands wider and the hit window tightens.

How to swing

↗ Topspin: low to high
→ Flat: across your body
↘ Slice: high to low

Shot type is inferred from wrist motion, not actual racket spin. No racket is required.
